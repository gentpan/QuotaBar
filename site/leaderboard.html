[[How it works, and how it stays honest||怎么算的，怎么防作弊]]

[[Track on QuotaBar, compete on Quota Run. The short version: nothing leaves your Mac until you say so, and nobody gets to report their own score.||QuotaBar 里看额度，Quota Run 上比速度。一句话：你不点头，什么都不出 Mac；谁也没法自己报成绩。]]

[[Local first||本地优先]]

[[Personal records need no account. QuotaBar keeps every run on your Mac — fastest to 100%, time to 50% and 90%, highest peak — whether or not you ever join.||个人纪录不用注册。最快用满、用到 50% 和 90% 的时间、最高峰值，QuotaBar 都记在你自己的 Mac 上，加不加入都一样。]]

[[Opt-in, in writing||先说清楚，再上传]]

[[Upload stays off until you join, and joining shows exactly what will leave the Mac before anything does. Leave Quota Run and every row about you is deleted from the server.||不加入就不上传。加入时先把会传出去的内容一条条列给你看，同意了才开始。退出 Quota Run，服务器上和你有关的数据全部删除。]]

[[Readings, not results||只传读数，不传成绩]]

[[The app sends raw readings; the server works out every run and every rank. A client that claims “100% in two hours” is simply ignored.||应用只上传原始读数，每一轮的成绩和名次都由服务器自己算。客户端声称「两小时用满」，服务器根本不看。]]

[[Uploaded after you join||加入后会上传]]

  • [[Provider and plan name||服务商和套餐名]]
  • [[Used percent, reset time and reading time for each window||每个额度窗口的已用百分比、重置时间、读取时间]]
  • [[The window's length and scope||窗口的长度和范围]]
  • [[A one-way digest of the provider account, never the email||服务商账号的单向摘要，不是邮箱本身]]
  • [[Tokens per minute from local CLI logs — counts only||本地 CLI 日志里每分钟的 token 数，只有数字]]

[[Never uploaded||绝不上传]]

  • [[Credentials, tokens, cookies, API keys||凭据、令牌、Cookie、API Key]]
  • [[Prompts, code, model outputs||提示词、代码、模型的输出]]
  • [[File and project paths||文件和项目路径]]
  • [[Your account email in the clear||明文的账号邮箱]]

[[One ranked Mac||只认一台 Mac]]

[[Each Mac signs its readings with a key that never leaves it, and an account ranks readings from one Mac only. Moving the ranking to another Mac waits seven days, and a provider account can belong to one runner.||每台 Mac 用一把不出本机的密钥给读数签名，一个账号只计其中一台的读数。换一台计分的 Mac 要等 7 天；同一个服务商账号只能归一个人。]]

[[Checked, then tiered||先核对，再分级]]

[[Readings have to rise steadily and plausibly. Runs with a gap, or a late start, count as Standard; readings that drop or leap, or an account seen under two runners, are held back for review and never shown.||读数得平稳、合理地往上走。有空档或者开始得晚的算「标准」；读数往回掉、突然猛跳，或者同一个账号出现在两个人名下，先扣下来复核，不上榜也不上主页。]]

[[No prizes||没有奖品]]

[[No money, no rewards. A desktop client can't be made impossible to cheat, only expensive and visible — so all there is to win is the bragging and a profile to show what you built.||不发钱，不发奖。桌面客户端做不到无法作弊，只能让作弊又费劲又显眼。能赢的只有面子，和一个展示你做了什么的主页。]]
