Quota Run

How Quota Run works

Quota Run ranks how fast people use up a subscription's quota window — a Codex week, a Claude five-hour window, a Cursor month. It is opt-in, run from QuotaBar on your Mac, and the server works out every result from signed readings. There are no prizes.

Joining

QuotaBar keeps personal records on your Mac with no account: every run, your fastest time to 100%, the time to 50% and 90%, and your highest peak. Nothing is uploaded for that.

  1. Open QuotaBar → Settings → Quota Run and choose “Sign in with quota.run”.
  2. Read the consent sheet. It lists exactly what will be uploaded, and nothing leaves the Mac until you agree.
  3. Your browser opens quota.run. Sign in with GitHub, Google or an email code — no passwords — check that the code matches the one in QuotaBar, and approve the Mac.

From then on QuotaBar uploads new readings after each refresh. Leaving is one button in the app or on your account page, and it deletes everything about you on the server.

What leaves your Mac

The app sends readings, not results: a client that claims “100% in two hours” would simply be ignored, because the server computes every run itself.

Uploaded after you join

  • Provider and plan name
  • Used percent, reset time and reading time for each quota window
  • The window's length and scope
  • A one-way digest of the provider account — never the email itself
  • Tokens per minute from local CLI logs — counts only

Never uploaded

  • Credentials, tokens, cookies and API keys
  • Prompts, code and model outputs
  • File and project paths
  • The provider account's email in the clear

Provider accounts: binding and ownership

QuotaBar reads which account each provider is signed in with and uploads only a digest of it. quota.run stores a keyed hash of that digest, so the email never leaves your Mac and can't be read back from the server.

Binding is required to rank
Readings without a provider account don't count, so a run only ranks when every reading carries one.
First upload binds it
Each provider account belongs to one Quota account: the first one to upload readings from it. Runs from the same provider account on anyone else's Quota account are held back.
Claiming by email
If the provider account's email is one of your verified sign-in emails on quota.run, it is yours — even if someone else uploaded it first. The server checks this without any extra upload; link that email under Account → Sign-in methods to claim it. A claim made this way can't be taken over.
Account verified
The mark next to a name when every reading of that run comes from a provider account claimed by email. It's a badge, not a tier: accounts under a different email still rank.
Unbinding
Unbinding a provider account on your account page deletes your readings and runs from it. A Mac still signed in to it binds it again on its next upload, unless you unbind it in QuotaBar too.

Tiers

Every run is checked when its readings arrive. Boards show two tiers; the “Verified only” switch hides the second.

Verified

  • Every reading comes from a provider account you own
  • Readings never drop more than 2 points
  • No jump of more than 60 points within 5 minutes
  • The first reading is at 50% or less, with no gap over 20 minutes until 100%
  • For Codex and Claude, real tokens in the local logs during the run

Standard

A believable run that misses the coverage or activity check — joined mid-window, say, or the Mac slept for a while.

Flagged

Readings that drop or leap, or a provider account owned by another Quota account. Kept for review and never shown on boards or profiles.

Unranked

A run with readings that carry no provider account. Stored, but never on a board, a profile best or recent runs.

Seasons and boards

A run
One reset period of one quota window for one provider account, from the window's start to its reset. Windows from 1 hour to 32 days rank.
A board
Everyone on the same provider, plan and window — Codex Pro 20x weekly, say. Each person appears once, with their best run.
Seasons
A season is an ISO week in UTC, counted by when the window started: 2026-W37. “This week” is the current one, “Last week” the one before, and “All time” takes each person's best from every season.
What's measured
Time from the window's start to the first reading at 100% (99.5% or more), 90% and 50%, and the highest peak. Ties on peak go to whoever got there first.
No prizes
No money and no rewards. A desktop client can't be made impossible to cheat, only expensive and visible — so all there is to win is the bragging, and a profile for what you build.

One ranked Mac

Each Mac signs its readings with a key that never leaves it — in the Secure Enclave when the Mac has one. An account can connect several Macs, but only the ranked one's readings count. Moving the ranking to another Mac waits 7 days after the last change; choosing one when there is none is always allowed.

Privacy

Public
Your username, display name, region, bio, links and projects; your results, their times and tiers; and each ranked run's usage curve — how the percentage rose over the window.
Never shown
Your sign-in email stays on quota.run to sign you in. It is never shown on a profile, a board or to anyone else. Provider account emails never reach the server at all.
Leaving
Deleting your account removes your profile, projects, readings, runs, Macs and sign-in methods from quota.run. QuotaBar stops uploading and keeps its local records.
